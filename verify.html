Check a Writing Record

Draft Punk is a writing app. As a writer works, it records how their text arrives: typed, pasted or imported. A writer can share that record with a link. Use this page to see a record, and to check whether a story you’ve received matches it. You don’t need an account.

WHAT THIS CHECK CAN AND CAN’T TELL YOU

The match compares the story with a fingerprint of the writer’s text, taken by Draft Punk when the link was issued. The fingerprint is made from short runs of words. It can confirm whether a story is the text in the record, but it can’t be turned back into the text.

The record shows how text was entered into Draft Punk: typed (on a keyboard, a phone keyboard or by dictation), pasted, or imported from a file. Draft Punk issues the report from the record saved in the writer’s account, and the writer can’t change the report itself. The figures are recorded on the writer’s own devices as they write.

It isn’t proof of authorship. Text from elsewhere can be retyped by hand. Treat the record as evidence of how a story was written, alongside your own judgement.
