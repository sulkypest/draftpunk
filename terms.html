2. THE SERVICE

Draft Punk is a place to draft fiction and track your writing: chapters, notes, beat notes and characters, word counts and goals, streaks and a game layer, Word Runner sprints, friends, messages and writing groups, and a Writing Record of how your text was entered.

Draft Punk is currently free. If we introduce paid plans, we will update these terms to cover payment, cancellation and refunds before anyone is asked to pay, and we will tell you in the app first.

We keep improving Draft Punk, so features may change, be added or be removed. If we ever plan to close Draft Punk, we will give you reasonable notice in the app so you have time to export your work.

3. WHO CAN USE DRAFT PUNK

You must be 13 or older to use Draft Punk.

When you create an account, or the first time you sign in after this section was added, we ask for the month and year you were born. Please answer truthfully. You can’t change it yourself afterwards; if you made a mistake, contact us.

Friends, messages, writing groups and chapter sharing are only for writers aged 16 and over. If you are under 16, you can use everything else in Draft Punk, and your writing is always yours to keep and export.

If you tell us you are under 13 when you create an account, the account can’t be used and we delete it. If we find out that a user is younger than they told us, we may restrict their account or close it, giving them the chance to export their writing first wherever we can.

4. SIGNING IN AND YOUR ACCOUNT

You can sign in with Google, with a code sent to your email address, or with an email address and password. Whichever you use, you are responsible for keeping access to it secure: your Google account, your email inbox, or your password.

You can also use Draft Punk without an account. If you do, your work is kept only in that browser on that device. It is not backed up or synced, and it can be lost if the browser’s data is cleared or the device is lost. Signing in keeps a copy in your account.

One account per person. Creating extra accounts to change leaderboard positions is not allowed.

5. YOUR WRITING

You own everything you write in Draft Punk: chapters, notes, beat notes, characters and messages. We claim no ownership of your work.

You give us permission to store, copy and sync your content only so that Draft Punk can work for you: to keep it on your devices and in your account, and to show it to the people you choose to share it with. We never use your writing for anything else. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI.

6. WHAT OTHER PEOPLE CAN SEE

  • Signed-in users can see your username and your word counts on leaderboards.
  • Friends and members of your writing groups can see your username and word counts, and the messages you send them.
  • Nobody can see your month and year of birth.
  • People you share a chapter with can read that chapter until you stop sharing it.
  • Anyone you give a Writing Record share link to can see that report (see section 9).

Everything else you write is private to you. The Privacy Policy has the details.

7. FRIENDS, MESSAGES AND GROUPS

These features are there to support writers, and are for writers aged 16 and over. You can only send a direct message to someone who has accepted you as a friend. Friend requests can only be sent to writers aged 18 and over; writers aged 16 and 17 can send requests but not receive them. You can report a message, friend request, shared chapter or writer, and block any writer, at any time: a blocked writer can’t message you or send you friend requests, and isn’t told. You can remove a friend, delete messages you have sent or received, leave a group, delete a group you created, and stop sharing a chapter, at any time. We may remove content, or suspend or close accounts, that misuse these features or break section 8.

9. WRITING RECORD

For each chapter, Draft Punk keeps a Writing Record of how text was entered: how much was typed (including by phone keyboard or dictation), pasted, dropped in or imported from a file, how much was deleted, and your active writing time. The text you paste is never recorded. The record shows how text arrived in Draft Punk. It does not, and cannot, prove who composed it.

Using AI or other tools in your writing process is your choice. If you share or rely on your Writing Record, you agree not to use it to claim you wrote something you did not, for example presenting text from an AI tool or another writer as your own work.

If you create a share link, anyone with the link can view that report: your project and chapter titles, your name or username, and the figures. Never your writing. You choose who to send it to, and you can stop sharing at any time. We make no promise that any publisher, competition, school or other organisation will accept a Writing Record as evidence.

10. STREAKS, LEVELS AND REWARDS

Streaks, freezes, XP, levels, bosses and buddies are part of the game in Draft Punk. They have no cash value and can’t be bought, sold or transferred. We may adjust how they work.

11. NOTIFICATIONS

Writing reminders and alerts for messages, friend requests and shared chapters are optional. You can turn them on or off in Account at any time.

12. LOOKING AFTER YOUR WORK

We take reasonable care to keep your work safe. When you are signed in, Draft Punk keeps a copy on your device and in your account, saves earlier versions of your chapters, and keeps a recovered copy if the same thing is changed on two devices. But no online service can promise never to lose data, and we cannot promise that Draft Punk will always be available.

Please keep your own copies of work that matters to you. Backup in the ··· menu saves everything on that device to a file you can restore later, and Export saves your story as a Word document, PDF or RTF.
