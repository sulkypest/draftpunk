PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. WHO WE ARE

Draft Punk is a writing app for novelists, made by Sulk Media. Sulk Media is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and your rights.

Contact: sulkmediaproduction@gmail.com

2. DATA WE COLLECT

Account data — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. If you sign in with an email address instead, we receive only that email address. If you choose a password, it is held by Google Firebase Authentication in protected (hashed) form; Draft Punk cannot see it. Firebase sends the emails for confirming your address and resetting your password.

Sign-in codes — If you ask for a sign-in code, it is emailed to you by Resend, our email delivery service, which receives your email address for that purpose only. We store the code and your address only in scrambled (hashed) form; a code works for 10 minutes and the record is deleted when it is used. We also count how many sign-in emails are sent each day, without any addresses.

Username — You choose a unique username when you first sign in. It is shown on leaderboards, to friends and in groups.

Your writing — Projects and goals, chapters, notes, beat notes, characters, Word Runner sprints and your streak are stored and synced to your account. This is private to you unless you share a chapter.

Word counts and progress — Your writing totals and your weekly, monthly and yearly counts, which power the tracker, leaderboards and groups.

Social data — Friend connections and friend requests, the messages you send and receive, and the writing groups you belong to, including group messages.

Shared chapters — If you share a chapter with a friend or a group, that chapter is stored so that they can read it until you stop sharing it.

Writing Record — For each chapter, a record of how text was entered: how much was typed, pasted, dropped in or imported from a file, how much was deleted, your active writing minutes each day, and the time and size of each paste. The text you paste is never recorded. A random identifier for each of your devices is stored so that records from different devices can be combined. This record is private to you unless you create a share link.

Writing Record share links — If you create a share link, a snapshot of your report (project and chapter titles, your username or Google display name, and the figures above, never your writing) is stored and can be viewed by anyone who has the link, without signing in, until you stop sharing or delete your account.

Notifications — Only if you turn them on: an identifier for each device you turn them on for (from Firebase Cloud Messaging), your reminder time, your time zone, and the date you were last reminded. Notifications never include message text or chapter titles.

Error and performance reports — When something goes wrong in the app, an error report is sent to Sentry: the error and where in the app it happened, the page, and your browser and device type. For about 1 in 10 visits, Sentry also receives timings of how quickly pages loaded. Sentry may receive your IP address with a report. Reports are not linked to your account.

On your device — Draft Punk keeps a copy of your work in your browser’s storage so it works offline, along with settings such as your theme and editor font. If you use Draft Punk without signing in, your work is stored only there.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your data only to run Draft Punk:

  • To provide the service you signed up for: your account and sign-in, syncing across your devices, leaderboards, friends, messages, groups and chapter sharing, your Writing Record and share links, and sign-in emails.
  • Notifications, only if you choose them: writing reminders and alerts. You can turn them off in Account at any time.
  • To keep Draft Punk working and secure: fixing errors, bot protection (reCAPTCHA), limits on sign-in codes, and simple counts such as how many accounts are created each week. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe, working service for these.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use your writing to train AI.

4. WHAT IS VISIBLE TO OTHERS

  • Signed-in users can see your username and word counts, the name and photo from your Google account if you sign in with Google, and the list of your friends’ accounts.
  • Friends and members of your groups can also see the messages you send them and any chapter you share with them.
  • Anyone with a Writing Record share link you create can see that report: titles, your name and the figures, never your writing.
  • Private: your writing, notes, beat notes, characters, Writing Record and email address are never visible to other users unless you share them as described above.

5. WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

Your account and writing are stored by Google Firebase in the United States. Error reports are stored by Sentry in the European Union (Germany), and sign-in codes are sent by Resend from its European region (Ireland). Where data is held outside the UK, these providers apply the safeguards UK data protection law requires.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Security rules control who can read each piece of data, as described in section 4.

6. SERVICES WE USE

  • Google Firebase — Sign-in, database, server functions and notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google reCAPTCHA — Bot prevention. Subject to Google’s Privacy Policy
  • Resend — Sends sign-in code emails.
  • Sentry — Error and performance reports. Sentry Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — Hosts the Draft Punk website.
  • jsDelivr — Supplies the code library that draws charts.

Like any website, GitHub Pages and the services that supply code to the app (Google, Sentry and jsDelivr) receive your IP address when your browser loads Draft Punk.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Get a copy of your data. Backup in the ··· menu saves your projects, chapters, notes and other work from that device as a file. For a copy of everything we hold about you, including messages and your Writing Record, email us.
  • Delete your account and its data, in the ··· menu → Account → Delete Account, or on our account deletion page.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, object to or restrict how we use your data, and take your data elsewhere (Backup and Export give you your work in standard formats).
  • Withdraw consent for notifications at any time, in Account.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you are unhappy with how we handle your data: ico.org.uk. We’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

For any of these, contact: sulkmediaproduction@gmail.com

8. HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, username, writing, notes, Writing Record, share links, shared chapters, the messages you sent or received, the groups you created and your messages in other groups from Draft Punk straight away. Copies held in our short-term database recovery backups expire within 30 days.

Error reports and email delivery logs are kept by Sentry and Resend for a limited period under their own policies. Copies on your own devices, including Backup and Export files, are yours to keep or delete.

9. CHILDREN

Draft Punk is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy as Draft Punk changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will tell you about significant changes in the app.

11. CONTACT

Questions about this policy: sulkmediaproduction@gmail.com
