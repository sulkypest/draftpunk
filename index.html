What is Draft Punk? ↓
01 · Write

Draft your novel, chapter by chapter.

A distraction-free editor with version history for every chapter. Export a standard manuscript as Word, PDF or RTF whenever you need it.

02 · Track & play

Your draft is the game.

Set a word goal and a deadline. Every word moves you along the map: a boss at each story beat, minions on the way, ranks as you level up. Daily streaks, streak freezes and reminders keep you going.

03 · Collect

100 buddies to collect.

Earn a new buddy for every 5,000 words you write, or rescue one by finishing a Word Runner sprint. Each of the seven sprint lengths, from 5 to 60 minutes, has its own set of buddies to find. Tap a buddy in your collection to meet them.

CollectionBuddies still to find shown as silhouettes
Word Runner

Pick a sprint, hit the word target before the clock runs out, and the buddy you were chasing joins your project.

  • 5minQuick Dash15 buddies
  • 10minShort Sprint15 buddies
  • 15minSteady Pace14 buddies
  • 20minPower Push14 buddies
  • 30minEndurance Run14 buddies
  • 45minMarathon Dash14 buddies
  • 60minGrand Sprint14 buddies
04 · Together

Write alongside friends.

Add writing friends, share chapters with them, and see who's written most on the weekly leaderboard.

05 · Writing Record

Show how your words arrived.

Draft Punk records how text came into each chapter: typed, dictated, pasted or imported. Export the record, or share it with a read-only link.

Your writing. Tracked. Anywhere.

Works in your browser on phone, tablet and computer, and adds to your home screen like an app. Your chapters are private to you, and you can export everything at any time.
